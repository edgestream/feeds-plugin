Changes: retain the original person and second contour; repeat the second
contour five units to the right as a third person/sound-wave contour.
Use white strokes and a padded square canvas.

feeds-people-waves.svg and .png have a transparent background.
feeds-people-waves-dark.svg and .png have an anthracite (#212121) background.
SVG files are editable sources; PNG files are 1024 x 1024 exports.

Small PNG exports append -64, -128, or -256 before .png, for both backgrounds.
They are rendered directly from the SVG sources, preserving the geometry.
Each small export is square and strictly below 10,000 bytes (the requested
project budget). The original 1024-pixel PNGs are not within this budget.

Verified file sizes in bytes (transparent / dark):
64 x 64: 1612 / 1462
128 x 128: 3462 / 2952
256 x 256: 7116 / 6058

OpenAI packaged branding image requirements checked on 2026-09-11:
https://developers.openai.com/plugins/deploy/submission-errors#image-errors
PNG is supported; raster branding images must be square, at least 48 x 48,
at most 4096 x 4096, and no larger than 5 MiB. The 10,000-byte export budget
is a stricter user requirement, not the limit stated on that page.
